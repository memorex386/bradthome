# Spec 039: states

The mocks are this slice's own build, captured on 2026-11-16 in Chromium at 1× density from a local preview of the production build by [capture.mjs](capture.mjs), with reduced motion. The card shots hide the sticky header and the floating "Ask about Brad", which would cover them; the no-script shots keep them. Once the slice lands, `tests/snapshots/039/` holds each state's baseline from the pinned container, and the pages on `next` are the record.

The approved direction is the private pictures study's picks of 2026-11-16: share cards, the apps' real icons, three Hopper diagrams, two small company marks, "bt_" in mono, and a photo by "Get in touch". Brad reviewed these at Gate 1 the same day, picked the recommended mark files and footer words, confirmed where the approval sits in the loop, chose the LinkedIn photo, and approved its crop.
