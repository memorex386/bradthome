# v2.7.0: how bradthome.com is built, the Capital One receipts and the essay's framing

bradthome.com moved from v2.6.0 to v2.7.0 on 2026-11-12 (UTC). The release puts three changes on live:

- **[Spec 034](../../specs/034-capital-one-receipts/spec.md), the Capital One receipts ([D219](../decisions/0219-the-capital-one-card-follows-linkedin-with-its-first-receipt.md)):**
  - The Hopper story's Capital One card follows Brad's LinkedIn wording.
  - Its first receipt links a 2024 travel-blog post showing the offer card and HTS's 2026 release. It claims only what they show.
  - RemoteUI reads "Co-architected and built".
  - The Mobile card adds two facts: joining on Android, and the move from Koin to Dagger/Hilt.
  - The résumé PDFs' margins and line height tighten, so each stays at two pages.
- **Slice 5.9, how bradthome.com is built ([spec 033](../../specs/033-site-build-story/spec.md), [D216](../decisions/0216-the-readme-describes-the-live-site.md)–[D218](../decisions/0218-the-design-system-gallery-is-linked-from-one-receipt.md)):**
  - bradthome.com gets a small card under "On the side", after Sudoku World, on the home and the role pages. It counts the repo's decision records, specs and releases at build and dates them by the build.
  - Each count, and each of the card's four claims, links to its records.
  - The résumé PDFs leave the card out.
  - The chat's corpus gains six facts about the site, and their project.
  - In the evals, the answer that writes the prompt cache may cost up to $0.01 ([D220](../decisions/0220-the-answer-that-writes-the-cache-may-cost-up-to-a-cent.md)).
  - A run's accuracy is judged across the chat's and interview prep's answers together ([D221](../decisions/0221-one-accuracy-bar-across-the-chat-and-interview-prep.md)). `/evals` shows both.
- **[Spec 035](../../specs/035-essay-and-judge-notes/spec.md), the essay's framing ([D222](../decisions/0222-the-essay-says-brad-reviews-and-approves-the-work.md)):**
  - The essay says AI agents write most of the code and Brad reviews and approves the work. This replaces "a one-person engineering team" and "the whole engineering team".
  - `/evals` shows the judge's notes with Brad's name wherever the judge wrote a pronoun.

Brad approved the release in three steps:

1. In the career session ("Yes, release").
2. On the version card in this session ("Release v2.7.0").
3. In the `production` environment.

The rehearsal before it is in [v2.7.0-rehearsal.md](v2.7.0-rehearsal.md), and the runbook is [docs/release.md](../release.md).

## The release

- **Tag:** `v2.7.0`, annotated and unsigned like v2.6.0, on main's `ea044a9`.
- **GitHub release:** [v2.7.0](https://github.com/memorex386/bradthome/releases/tag/v2.7.0).
- **Before the tag:** main's `ci`, `next` and CodeQL passed on `ea044a9`.
- **Run:** [release 43235722527](https://github.com/memorex386/bradthome/actions/runs/43235722527), with `words-may-change=home-and-essay`:
  - spec 034 and slice 5.9 change the home's words, and spec 035 changes the essay's;
  - the check compares no other page's words.
- **Stage:** the stage job put version `e6cff473b9b19493` on the `release` channel. Its pre-release check against v2.6.0 on bradthome.com printed:


- **Release:** Brad approved the release job in the `production` environment. It printed:

  ```text
  ✓ release  version e6cff473b9b19493 is served on live at https://bradthome.com: 39 files, after 4 s
  ```

- **Status:** `pnpm release:status` then read `live (https://bradthome.com) serves version e6cff473b9b19493, released 2026-11-12T14:32:48.370Z: v2.7.0 (ea044a9)`. A rollback would put back v2.6.0's `55eb8420929a08ec`.

The chat API wasn't redeployed. It answers from the corpus it bundled when it was last deployed, so its answers gain the new content only after `pnpm chat-api:deploy`:

- the site's facts;
- spec 034's claims;
- the essay's new lines.

That deploy waits for Brad's own go-ahead.

Each change's eval run ran in CI before it landed: 43107162574 for spec 034, 43150483510 for slice 5.9 and 43224635686 for spec 035. `/evals` shows the last of them.

After the release, four evals branches were deleted, as [D193](../decisions/0193-eval-runs-start-from-an-evals-branch.md) says: `evals/032-capital-one-receipts`, `evals/034-capital-one-receipts`, `evals/033-site-build-story` and `evals/035-essay-and-judge-notes`.
