# v2.11.0: the job history in date order

bradthome.com moved from v2.10.0 to v2.11.0 on 2026-11-17 (UTC). The release puts one change on live:

- **Slice 5.13, the job history in date order ([spec 040](../../specs/040-job-order/spec.md), [D238](../decisions/0238-realpage-gets-a-card-in-date-order.md)):**
  - The Experience section's cards read newest first: Bleacher Report, RealPage, BookShout, then Bottle Rocket. RealPage, a dated line until now, is a card with its sentence and a Kotlin · Java · RxJava tag row, under the kicker "Real-estate apps", on the home and each role page.
  - The heading reads "Android for brands, a startup, real-estate apps and a sports media platform".
  - The résumé PDFs print RealPage's sentence and stay at two pages each, with tighter page margins and gaps.
  - `/corpus.json` and `/llms.txt` link RealPage's sentence to its claim, `#receipt-realpage-2`, and add the stack it names, `realpage-3`, at `#experience-realpage`.

Brad answered "Yes, release" in the career session, then chose "Release v2.11.0" on a card in spec 040's session, which covered the rehearsal, the tag, the release run and approving it in the `production` environment from Brad's account. The runbook is [docs/release.md](../release.md).
