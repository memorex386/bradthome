# v2.5.0: "Ask about Brad" in reach on a phone

bradthome.com moved from v2.4.0 to v2.5.0 on 2026-11-11 (UTC). The release puts slice 5.7 on live:

- [spec 031](../../specs/031-ask-on-phones/spec.md): on a phone, a corner "Ask about Brad" button shows whenever the hero's is off screen, and the hero's is hidden meanwhile, so the chat is one tap away anywhere on the page. It first rises in at 1.8 s, once the hero has settled. While the visitor scrolls down it shrinks to a 56 px circle around its sparkle, and it opens on a stop or a scroll up. It hides at the contact section ([D214](../decisions/0214-ask-about-brad-floats-on-phones.md)).

Motion plays only when the visitor allows it. Brad approved the release in the career session ("Yes, release"), and again in the `production` environment. The rehearsal before it is in [v2.5.0-rehearsal.md](v2.5.0-rehearsal.md), and the runbook is [docs/release.md](../release.md).
