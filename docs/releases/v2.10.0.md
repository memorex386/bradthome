# v2.10.0: the pictures

bradthome.com moved from v2.9.0 to v2.10.0 on 2026-11-16 (UTC). The release puts one change on live:

- **Slice 5.12, the pictures ([spec 039](../../specs/039-pictures/spec.md), [D232](../decisions/0232-share-cards-drawn-at-build.md)–[D237](../decisions/0237-brads-photo-by-get-in-touch.md)):**
  - The home, the three role pages, the essay and `/evals` each have a 1200 × 630 share card, drawn at build from the page's own words. A link to any of them unfurls with its card, as `summary_large_image`.
  - VINDecoded's and Sudoku World's side cards show their App Store icons, and bradthome.com's card shows the site icon.
  - The Hopper band shows three diagrams, for Capital One, RemoteUI and the agent workflow. Every label is made of its claim's words, and the build checks it.
  - Hopper's mark sits beside the band's eyebrow and Bleacher Report's beside its card's title. The footer of the home and role pages says the companies' names and marks belong to their owners.
  - The site icon is "bt_", with a 180 px home-screen icon.
  - Brad's photo sits by "Get in touch".

Brad chose "Release v2.10.0" on a card in this session, and approved the release in the `production` environment. The runbook is [docs/release.md](../release.md).

## The release

- **Tag:** `v2.10.0`, annotated and unsigned like v2.9.0, on main's `0771a9c`.
- **GitHub release:** [v2.10.0](https://github.com/memorex386/bradthome/releases/tag/v2.10.0).
- **Before the tag:** main's `ci`, `next` and CodeQL passed on `0771a9c`, with no open code-scanning alert.
- **Rehearsal:** the candidate was released on a preview channel and rolled back there to v2.9.0's version, which then served bradthome.com's bytes ([rehearsal](v2.10.0-rehearsal.md)).
- **Run:** [release 43945428691](https://github.com/memorex386/bradthome/actions/runs/43945428691), with `words-may-change=home`. The Capital One diagram's labels are words on the home. Of the other pages, the check compares only the essay's words, which didn't change.
- **Stage:** the stage job put version `0c95b3186fa8ffff` on the `release` channel. Its pre-release check against v2.9.0 on bradthome.com printed:

  ```text
  – check  words differ on /: live has “to Capital One customers. Sources The offer card, in a 2024 travel-blog”, candidate has “to Capital One customers. Sole engineering owner RFC and ADR, stakeholder alignment” (this run allows it)
  ✓ check  https://bradthome--release-gn6n25b2.web.app serves every URL, redirect, security header and word that https://bradthome.com does (31 URLs, 2 pages' words)
  ```

- **Release:** Brad approved the release job in the `production` environment. It printed:

  ```text
  ✓ release  version 0c95b3186fa8ffff is served on live at https://bradthome.com: 52 files, after 6 s
  ```

- **Status:** `pnpm release:status` then read `live (https://bradthome.com) serves version 0c95b3186fa8ffff, released 2026-11-16T20:11:18.571Z: v2.10.0 (0771a9c)`. A rollback would put back v2.9.0's `7e055f3009c6d7ff`.

The chat API wasn't redeployed, and needs no redeploy for this release: `/corpus.json` and `/llms.txt` are byte for byte v2.9.0's. No eval case changed, so no eval run was needed (D228).
