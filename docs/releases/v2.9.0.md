# v2.9.0: proof on the card, and a sparkle to ask about any claim

bradthome.com moved from v2.8.0 to v2.9.0 on 2026-11-13 (UTC). The release puts one change on live:

- **Slice 5.11, proof on the card ([spec 038](../../specs/038-proof-on-the-card/spec.md), [D229](../decisions/0229-proof-on-the-card.md)–[D231](../decisions/0231-the-charter-drops-every-claim-links-to-its-evidence.md)):**
  - The home and role pages drop the numbered receipts and their drawers, with every "Ask me" and "No public link" line.
  - Bleacher Report's 10M+ links its Google Play listing, and VINDecoded's 1M+ and 400K+ link Google Play and the App Store, each with its host under the label.
  - The Capital One card lists its two sources under its claims, and bradthome.com's card its four, each a labeled link with its host. The cards keep their own links, so all 13 public sources show at rest and each opens in one tap.
  - Each of the 30 claims ends with the Ask button's sparkle. A tap opens Ask about Brad with the claim's words in its box, unsent; with no script it reaches Contact.
  - Each claim keeps the anchor its receipt had, `#receipt-<claim id>`, so every chat source link still lands on its claim, which tints for a moment.
  - The charter drops its first product principle, "Every claim links to its evidence".

Brad asked in this session to deploy to production, and approved the release in the `production` environment. The runbook is [docs/release.md](../release.md).
