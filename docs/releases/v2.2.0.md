# v2.2.0: interview prep goes live

bradthome.com moved from v2.1.0 to v2.2.0 on 2026-11-09 (UTC), which puts interview prep in "Ask about Brad" on live: a visitor pastes a job posting or names a role, and gets Brad's fit, with each match's sources in their own words, the requirements the site doesn't show, and questions to ask Brad ([spec 024](../../specs/024-interview-prep/spec.md), [D201](../decisions/0201-prep-answers-as-checked-json-with-a-source-for-each-match.md)–[D203](../decisions/0203-prep-shows-its-sources-words-and-unmatched-requirements.md)). It's the last slice of phase 4. The rehearsal before it is in [v2.2.0-rehearsal.md](v2.2.0-rehearsal.md), and the runbook is [docs/release.md](../release.md).

## The evals

The run on live is [42698177725](https://github.com/memorex386/bradthome/actions/runs/42698177725), on `663a92e`, with Haiku 5.5 answering and Sonnet 5.5 judging: 63 of 63 cases safe, the chat's accuracy 24 of 26 and prep's 11 of 12, each within its own allowance ([D200](../decisions/0200-safety-must-pass-every-case-accuracy-95-percent.md), spec 024's S7.4), for $0.22. Prep's answers cost $0.0009 at most in the run, and a cold one $0.0039 on production, against the $0.01 limit.

The slice took six runs, about $1.38 of the `bradthome-evals` workspace's $5, which has now spent about $2.22. Runs 1 to 3 held prep at 3 or 4 of 12 judged supported, with model-written gaps and evidence the misses. Brad then chose to ground both by construction (D203), and runs 4 to 6 passed prep at 12, 12 and 11 of 12.
