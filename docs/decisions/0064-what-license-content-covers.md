# What LICENSE-CONTENT covers

## Context and problem statement

**The question:** What counts as content that Brad keeps all rights to?

Applies [D42](0042-mit-for-code-all-rights-reserved-for-content.md) and [D54](0054-husky-gitleaks-and-two-license-files.md). The site's pages are Brad's own writing. Keeping the process docs reusable fits the repo's role as a work sample.

## Considered options

The options are listed in the builder's ranked order.

1. The site's words and images (recommended, chosen)
2. The site's words and images, plus the docs, specs and decision records

## Decision outcome

Chosen option: "The site's words and images".

LICENSE-CONTENT covers the site's words and images: everything that becomes a page, image or PDF on bradthome.com is all rights reserved. Docs, specs and decision records stay MIT with the code.
