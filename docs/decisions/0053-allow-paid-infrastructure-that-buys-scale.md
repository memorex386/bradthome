# Allow paid infrastructure that buys scale

## Context and problem statement

**The question:** Change the charter to allow small paid infrastructure

Brad's note on hosting (R5): a few dollars a month is fine. This changes [D32](0032-non-goals.md) and the reading of [D34](0034-no-fixed-spending-ceiling.md) confirmed in [D36](0036-approve-the-charter.md).

## Considered options

The options are listed in the builder's ranked order.

1. Change the charter as worded (recommended, chosen)
2. Change it, with a monthly ceiling I name below
3. Keep the non-goal

## Decision outcome

Chosen option: "Change the charter as worded".

Charter change: the non-goal "No paid infrastructure before the chat" is removed. Paid infrastructure is fine when it buys scalability or avoids a later migration. Each paid service is its own decision, with a spending cap, monitoring and, for AI features, evals.
