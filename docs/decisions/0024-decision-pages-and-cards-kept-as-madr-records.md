# Decision pages and cards, kept as MADR records

## Context and problem statement

**The question:** Where does Brad make decisions, and where are they kept?

Principles 5 and 6. The record has to be versioned and to go public with the repo.

## Considered options

The options are listed in the builder's ranked order.

1. Decision pages and cards, kept as MADR (recommended, chosen)
2. GitHub issues with artifacts, kept as MADR
3. Chat cards only, kept as MADR

## Decision outcome

Chosen option: "Decision pages and cards, kept as MADR".

**Decisions are made in two places:** decision pages for big batches, and chat question cards for quick ones.

Each tier-1 decision becomes a MADR 4.0 record in `docs/decisions`. The decision log indexes every tier.
