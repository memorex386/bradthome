# Three tracks for three sizes of change

## Context and problem statement

**The question:** How much process does each change get?

Overhead is the main complaint about spec-driven work. Brad asked for extra time so the agent can build navigable mock UI, UX and flows in a canvas or artifact whenever they apply.

## Considered options

The options are listed in the builder's ranked order.

1. Three tracks (recommended, chosen)
2. Two tracks
3. One full track

## Decision outcome

Chosen option: "Three tracks".

**Three tracks.**
- Full: a feature or slice.
- Light: a small change with clear criteria.
- Trivial: copy, typos and docs.

The agent proposes a track and Brad confirms it.

Full-track work with anything visible, or with a multi-step flow, gets navigable mocks, flows, graphs and visuals in a canvas or artifact. They're reviewed together with the spec.
