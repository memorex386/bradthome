# Reword the charter's purpose

## Context and problem statement

**The question:** How does the public charter describe who the site is for?

The earlier wording named a narrower audience. The new wording covers the same three kinds of visitors as the charter's Audience section.

## Considered options

The options are listed in the builder's ranked order.

1. For anyone who wants to see how Brad works (recommended, chosen)
2. Keep the earlier wording

## Decision outcome

Chosen option: "For anyone who wants to see how Brad works".

Charter change: the Purpose says bradthome.com is a résumé you can explore, for anyone who wants to see how Brad works, from a recruiter's first skim to an engineer reading the code.
