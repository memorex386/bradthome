# Non-goals

## Context and problem statement

**The question:** Approve the non-goals

They keep scope from creeping.

## Considered options

The options are listed in the builder's ranked order.

1. Approve all (recommended, chosen)
2. Approve all except the ones I name below
3. Discuss each one

## Decision outcome

Chosen option: "Approve all".

The non-goals are approved as listed:
- no VINDecoded marketing
- no accounts, comments, newsletter or CMS
- no tracking that identifies visitors, and no ad scripts
- no private facts or phone number
- no blog platform
- no paid infrastructure before the chat
