# No target date

## Context and problem statement

**The question:** Is there a date the relaunch should hit?

Claude recommended a target date with scope cut first, and Brad chose no date (option 2). The risk is drift. The mitigation is small slices, each of which ships something, ordered must-haves first in step 0.4.

## Considered options

The options are listed in the builder's ranked order.

1. A target date, and scope bends first (recommended)
2. No date (chosen)
3. A hard date, and anything bends

## Decision outcome

Chosen option: "No date".

No target date. Quality comes first, and the relaunch ships when its slices are done. Today's site stays live until the cutover.
