# Purposeful motion

## Context and problem statement

**The question:** Approve the product principles

Brad mostly agreed with calm motion, and suggested that interactions or scrolling could trigger other moments. The rewritten wording went to Brad with the charter.

## Considered options

The options are listed in the builder's ranked order.

1. Approve all (recommended)
2. Approve all except the ones I name below (chosen)
3. Discuss each one

## Decision outcome

Chosen option: "Approve all except the ones I name below".

The product principles are approved, with calm motion rewritten from Brad's note. A page gets one orchestrated moment when it opens. After that, motion responds to what the visitor does: hovering, tapping, or scrolling a section into view. It stays short, never loops, never hides content, and turns off with reduced motion.
